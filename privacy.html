Global Brief Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Global Brief Studio is a personal video tool used only by the owner of the Global Brief News channel, on the owner's own computer.

1. What information is used

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the tool receives from TikTok:

  • basic profile information: display name, profile picture and account ID (scope user.info.basic)
  • permission to upload videos to the owner's drafts (scope video.upload)
  • sign-in tokens that keep the connection working

2. How it is used

This information is used only to show which account is connected and to send the owner's own videos to that account's TikTok drafts.

3. Where it is stored

Everything is stored only on the owner's own computer. There is no server, no database, and no cloud copy. The tool does not use analytics, tracking or advertising.

4. Sharing

No information is sold, rented or shared with anyone. Data is only sent to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to upload drafts.

5. Other people's data

The tool does not collect information about other TikTok users or viewers.

6. Deleting your data

Clicking "Disconnect" in the tool deletes the stored TikTok sign-in. Access can also be removed at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services (or Manage app permissions).

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.
